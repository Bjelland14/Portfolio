About me

I'm a frontend development student at Noroff, finishing in December 2026. I work mainly with React, TypeScript and REST APIs, and I'm growing into fullstack with C# and .NET through a Pluralsight course. I use AI tools like Claude to debug and solve problems, and I document how I use them.
